# Rooms and apartments

All rooms and apartments are non-smoking and have a private bathroom with shower, hairdryer,
towels and bed linen, free Wi-Fi, heating, air conditioning, a safe and a small fridge. There is no
TV in the rooms; a shared living room with TV and a library is in the main farmhouse.

## Ulivo (double room)

Ground floor of the main farmhouse, 18 m², garden view. One double bed (160x200 cm), sleeps up to
2 guests. Step-free access from the parking area and a walk-in shower with grab bars: this is our
accessible room. Dogs are allowed in Ulivo. A cot for an infant can be added on request.

## Cipresso (double or twin room)

First floor of the main farmhouse, 17 m², view of the cypress avenue. Can be set up as one double
bed or two single beds (please tell us when booking). Sleeps up to 2 guests. Reached by an internal
staircase, no lift.

## Girasole (triple room)

First floor, 22 m², view over the olive grove. One double bed and one single bed, sleeps up to 3
guests. Reached by stairs, no lift.

## Vigna (family room)

First floor, 28 m², view over the vineyard. One double bed and two single beds in a separate
alcove, sleeps up to 4 guests. An extra bed can be added for a fifth person (see Prices), but the
room becomes quite crowded; for 5 or more guests we usually suggest the Granaio apartment.

## Torre (junior suite)

Top two floors of the old watchtower, 30 m², with a private terrace and a 360-degree view towards
San Gimignano. One king-size bed, sitting area, bathtub and shower. Sleeps 2 guests; an extra bed
can be added for a third person. The tower is reached by a narrow spiral staircase and is not
suitable for guests with reduced mobility or for children under 6.

## Fienile (apartment, up to 4 guests)

The former hayloft, a separate single-storey building, 45 m². One bedroom with a double bed, a
living room with a double sofa bed, kitchenette (two induction hobs, microwave, fridge, kettle,
moka pot), private fenced garden. Step-free access. Dogs are allowed in Fienile.

## Granaio (apartment, up to 6 guests)

The old granary, 80 m² on two floors. Two double bedrooms (one can be twin), a sofa bed for two in
the living room, two bathrooms, full kitchen with oven and dishwasher, washing machine, outdoor
dining area with barbecue. Air conditioning is in the bedrooms only; the living area has ceiling
fans.

## Cots and children

A cot for children under 3 is free in every room and apartment, on request. High chairs are
available in the restaurant and in both apartments.

## Housekeeping

Rooms are cleaned daily, with towels changed every two days or on request. Apartments are cleaned
before arrival and then once a week, with a change of towels and bed linen; extra cleaning can be
requested for 25 EUR.
