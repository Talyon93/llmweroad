# Policies

## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in: from 15:00 to 20:00.
- Check-out: by 10:30.
- Late arrival after 20:00 is possible only if agreed in advance; we leave the keys in a key box
  and send the instructions by message. No arrivals after 23:00.
- Early check-in depends on the room being ready and cannot be guaranteed. Luggage can always be
  left at reception from 9:00, and guests can use the garden and pool while they wait.
- Late check-out until 13:00 may be possible on request for €25, depending on the next arrivals.

## Deposit and payment

A deposit of 30% of the accommodation price is required to confirm a booking, paid by bank
transfer or credit card within 3 days of our offer. The balance is paid at check-out. We accept
cash, Visa, Mastercard, Maestro and bank transfer. American Express and cheques are not accepted.

## Cancellation

- Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival: the deposit is refunded in full.
- Cancellation 13 days or less before arrival: 50% of the total accommodation price is charged.
- No-show or departure before the end of the booked stay: 100% of the booked stay is charged.
- Date changes are possible up to 14 days before arrival, subject to availability.

Refunds and any exception to these rules are handled personally by Giulia Bruni. We recommend
travel insurance.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome in the Ulivo room and the Fienile apartment only, one dog per unit, for €15 per
night. Dogs must be on a leash in the common areas and are not allowed in the restaurant, the
breakfast room or the pool area. Please bring your dog's bed and bowls. Other animals (cats, birds,
etc.) must be agreed with us in advance. Assistance dogs are welcome in every unit and are free of
charge.

## Children

Children of all ages are welcome. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool
area. The Torre suite is not suitable for children under 6 because of the spiral staircase.

## Smoking

Smoking is not allowed inside any building. Guests may smoke in the garden, away from the pool and
the restaurant terrace; ashtrays are provided.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 23:00 to 8:00. Music outdoors is not allowed in the evening except during
private events booked with us.

## Visitors

Day visitors who are not staying at CasaVaiano cannot use the pool. Friends visiting guests are
welcome in the common areas until 22:00 and can book dinner at the restaurant.

## Damages and house rules

Guests are responsible for damage caused during their stay. Parties, drones and fireworks are not
allowed unless organised with us as a private event.

## Privacy

We only use guests' personal data to manage bookings and as required by Italian law (guests must
show an ID document at check-in for police registration).
