# Prices and seasons

## Seasons

| Season | Dates |
|---|---|
| Low | 1 March to 31 March, 3 November to 19 December |
| Mid | 1 April to 14 June, 16 September to 2 November |
| High | 15 June to 15 September, and the Christmas period 20 December to 6 January |

The property is closed from 7 January to the end of February. Each night is charged at the price of
its own season, so a stay across two seasons mixes the two prices.

## Nightly prices

Prices are in EUR per unit per night, VAT included. Room prices include breakfast for all guests in
the room. Apartment prices do not include breakfast.

| Unit | Type | Max guests | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ulivo | Double room | 2 | €95 | €115 | €140 |
| Cipresso | Double or twin room | 2 | €95 | €115 | €140 |
| Girasole | Triple room | 3 | €120 | €145 | €175 |
| Vigna | Family room | 4 | €140 | €170 | €210 |
| Torre | Junior suite | 2 | €150 | €185 | €230 |
| Fienile | Apartment | 4 | €130 | €160 | €200 |
| Granaio | Apartment | 6 | €180 | €220 | €280 |

The price is per unit, not per person: a couple in Vigna pays the same as a family of four.

## Minimum stay

The minimum stay depends on the season of the arrival date.

| Unit type | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rooms | 1 night | 2 nights | 3 nights |
| Apartments | 3 nights | 3 nights | 7 nights |

Shorter stays may sometimes be possible at the last minute; please ask and we will check.

## Extras

- Extra bed (Vigna and Torre only): €30 per night.
- Dog: €15 per night (Ulivo and Fienile only, see Policies).
- Breakfast for apartment guests: €12 per person per day, served in the farmhouse breakfast room.
- Cot for children under 3: free.

## Long-stay discount

Stays of 7 nights or more get 10% off the accommodation price. The discount does not apply to
extras, the tourist tax or restaurant dinners.

## Tourist tax

The municipality of Gambassi Terme charges a tourist tax of €1.50 per person per night, for a
maximum of 7 consecutive nights. Children under 14 are exempt. The tax is not included in our
prices and is paid in cash or by card at check-out.
