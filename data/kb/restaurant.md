# Restaurant and breakfast

## Breakfast

Breakfast is served every day from 8:00 to 10:00 in the breakfast room or, in good weather, on the
terrace. It is included for room guests. Apartment guests can add it for €12 per person per day.
It is a buffet with homemade cakes and jams, bread, local cheese and cured meats, eggs on request,
yogurt, fruit, juice, coffee and tea. Gluten-free bread and plant-based milk are always available.

## Dinner

Our restaurant serves dinner from Thursday to Sunday, 19:30 to 22:00. It is closed on Monday,
Tuesday and Wednesday. Lunch is not served.

Dinner is a four-course Tuscan set menu at €38 per person, drinks excluded. Children under 10 pay
€20. The menu changes every week and uses vegetables from our garden, our own olive oil and meat
from local farms. Our own red wine is €22 per bottle; a glass of house wine is €5.

Reservation is required, also for guests staying at CasaVaiano, by 12:00 on the same day. The
restaurant is open to people who are not staying with us, subject to availability. Indoors seats
40 people; the terrace seats 30 (May to September).

## Diets and allergies

A vegetarian alternative is always available. Vegan and gluten-free menus are possible if
requested at least 48 hours in advance. We take allergies seriously, but our kitchen is small and
handles flour, nuts and dairy every day: we cannot guarantee the absence of traces. Guests with
coeliac disease or severe allergies should contact us before booking so that Paolo can discuss the
menu with them personally.

## On non-dinner days

On Monday to Wednesday we can recommend restaurants in Gambassi Terme (3 km) and Certaldo (12 km).
Apartment guests can also cook in their kitchen. On request we prepare a picnic basket (€18 per
person, order the day before).

## Group dinners

Birthday or family dinners for up to 20 people can be booked on restaurant days with the regular
set menu. For more than 20 people, or for a private menu, see Events.
