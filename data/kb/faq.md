# Frequently asked questions

## Is CasaVaiano suitable for guests with reduced mobility?

Partly. The Ulivo room and the Fienile apartment have step-free access and the Ulivo bathroom has
a walk-in shower with grab bars. The breakfast room and the restaurant are on the ground floor and
step-free. The other rooms are reached by stairs and there is no lift. The garden paths are gravel.
There is no lift or hoist into the pool.

## Do you have air conditioning?

Yes, in all rooms and in the Fienile apartment. In the Granaio apartment air conditioning is in the
bedrooms only, and the living area has ceiling fans.

## Is there a supermarket nearby?

Yes, a small supermarket and a pharmacy are in Gambassi Terme (3 km). Larger supermarkets are in
Castelfiorentino (12 km).

## Can we arrive by bike or on foot from the Via Francigena?

Yes, many pilgrims stay with us. Bikes can be stored in our closed barn. A one-night stay in a room
is possible outside the high season (see Minimum stay).

## Can you organise excursions or wine tours?

We are happy to suggest itineraries and give contacts of local wineries and licensed guides, but we
do not sell tours ourselves. Bookings with third parties are paid directly to them.

## Can I have a gift voucher?

Yes, we sell gift vouchers for stays, dinners and the cooking class. Contact Giulia by email.

## Is the water drinkable?

Yes, tap water is drinkable. We also provide a free bottle of water in each room on arrival.

## Do you have a gym or a spa?

No. We have a pool, gardens and walking trails. The thermal baths in Gambassi Terme are a short
drive away, and a massage therapist can come to the property on request.

## Can we get married at CasaVaiano?

Yes, see Weddings and events. Every wedding needs a personal proposal from Giulia.

## Are there mosquitoes?

In summer, a few. All windows have mosquito nets.

## Is the property suitable for a quiet holiday?

Yes, we are surrounded by countryside and the nearest road is 800 m away. On a few summer weekends
there may be a private event in the garden; we always inform guests in advance.

## Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Apartment guests can of course cook. Room guests can use the picnic tables in the garden. Outside
food and drinks are not allowed in the restaurant.

## What happens if it rains during my stay?

The library and the living room are always open, the cooking class and tasting are indoors, and
Florence, Siena, San Gimignano and Volterra have plenty of museums.
