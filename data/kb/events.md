# Weddings, parties and private events

## What we can host

CasaVaiano hosts weddings, civil ceremony receptions, anniversaries, birthdays and company retreats.
Large events take place in the garden under the olive trees (up to 120 seated guests) or in the
restaurant hall (up to 60 seated guests in case of rain). Overnight accommodation is limited to our
23 beds; we can suggest other hotels nearby for additional guests.

## Season and days

Weddings and events with more than 40 guests are possible from May to mid-October. Smaller private
dinners and parties are possible during the whole opening period. Outdoor music must end at 24:00
for all events.

## Exclusive use

For weddings we require exclusive use of the whole property for at least two nights, so that the
couple and their families occupy all rooms and apartments. Some dates for the coming season are
already reserved for events.

## Catering and suppliers

The catering is prepared by our kitchen (Tuscan menus, buffet or served dinner). Prices for event
menus start from €75 per person and depend on the menu, drinks and number of guests. We work with
trusted local florists, photographers and musicians; couples may also bring their own suppliers.
External caterers are not allowed.

## Ceremonies

Civil wedding ceremonies have legal value only at the town hall of Gambassi Terme, a 5-minute drive
away. Symbolic ceremonies can be held in the garden. Religious ceremonies are held in the nearby
parish churches by arrangement with the local parish.

## How to enquire

Every event is a custom proposal. Please tell us the date (or period), number of guests, type of
event and your budget. Giulia handles all event requests personally and will reply with a proposal
and to arrange a visit. The assistant cannot confirm event dates, prices or menus.
