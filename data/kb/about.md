# About CasaVaiano

## Who we are

CasaVaiano is a family-run agriturismo on a hill just outside Gambassi Terme, in the province of
Florence, Tuscany. The farm has been in the Bruni family for three generations. Today it is run by
Giulia Bruni (bookings, guests and events) and her brother Paolo Bruni (kitchen and farm).

The property is a restored 18th-century stone farmhouse with an old watchtower, a former hayloft
and a granary, surrounded by 12 hectares of olive groves, a small Sangiovese vineyard and woods.
We produce our own extra-virgin olive oil and a small amount of red wine.

## Accommodation at a glance

We have five rooms in the main farmhouse (Ulivo, Cipresso, Girasole, Vigna, Torre) and two
self-catering apartments in separate buildings (Fienile and Granaio). The property can host up to
23 guests overnight.

## Opening period

We are open from 1 March to 6 January. The property is closed from 7 January to the end of
February for maintenance and olive tree pruning. During the closure we cannot accept stays, events
or restaurant bookings, but emails are still answered.

## How booking works

Guests can send a booking request by email, WhatsApp or the chat on our website with dates, number
of guests (and children's ages) and the preferred room. We reply with availability and a quote.
A booking is confirmed only after the deposit is received (see Policies). The assistant can check
availability and prepare a quote, but only the Bruni family can confirm a reservation.
