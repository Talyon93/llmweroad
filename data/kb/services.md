# Services and activities

## Swimming pool

Outdoor saltwater pool (12 x 6 m, depth 1.2 to 1.6 m) with sun loungers and umbrellas, open from
15 May to 30 September, 9:00 to 19:00, weather permitting. There is no lifeguard. Pool towels are
provided. The pool is reserved for staying guests. There is no lift or hoist into the pool.

## Wi-Fi and working

Free Wi-Fi (fibre, about 100 Mbps) in all rooms, apartments and common areas. The library in the
farmhouse has a quiet desk for remote work.

## Parking and electric cars

Free private parking inside the property, not guarded. One electric car charger (22 kW, Type 2) is
available on request at €0.45 per kWh.

## Cooking class

Pasta-making class with Paolo on Tuesday and Saturday at 16:00, about 3 hours, followed by dinner
with what you cooked. €65 per person including dinner and wine. Minimum 4 participants, maximum
10. Book at least 2 days ahead. Available from April to October.

## Wine and olive oil tasting

Tasting of our olive oil and wine with local cheese and cured meats, every Wednesday at 18:00,
€25 per person, about 1.5 hours. Our olive oil (0.5 litre, €14) and wine (€22) can be bought at
reception.

## Bikes and walks

E-bike rental at €35 per day (helmet included), 4 bikes available, book in advance. Regular bikes
are free for guests. The Via Francigena walking route passes about 2 km from the property; we have
printed maps of local loops from 5 to 18 km.

## Laundry

Laundry service: €10 per load (wash and dry), ready the same evening if handed in by 10:00.
The Granaio apartment has its own washing machine.

## Massages

A local massage therapist can come to the property on request (60 minutes, €70), booked at least
one day in advance. The thermal baths in the village of Gambassi Terme are a 5-minute drive; they
are not run by us.

## Transfers

We do not run a shuttle service. We can book a local taxi for you: indicative prices are about
€110 from Florence airport, €150 from Pisa airport and €30 from Castelfiorentino or Certaldo
train stations. The taxi is paid directly to the driver.
