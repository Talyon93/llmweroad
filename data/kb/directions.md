# How to reach us

## Address

CasaVaiano, Via di Vaiano 12, 50050 Gambassi Terme (FI), Italy. The property is 3 km from the
village centre of Gambassi Terme along a well-kept gravel road (the last 800 m). Normal cars can
drive it without problems; drive slowly.

## By car

A car is strongly recommended: public transport in the area is limited.

- From Florence (about 55 km, 55 minutes): take the FI-PI-LI dual carriageway towards Pisa/Livorno,
  exit at Empoli, follow the signs for Castelfiorentino and then Gambassi Terme. In Gambassi Terme
  follow the brown signs "CasaVaiano".
- From Pisa airport (about 85 km, 1 hour 10 minutes): FI-PI-LI towards Florence, exit at San
  Miniato, then follow the signs for Castelfiorentino and Gambassi Terme.
- From Siena (about 60 km, 1 hour): towards Poggibonsi, then Certaldo and Gambassi Terme.

GPS navigators sometimes suggest a steep dirt track from the north: ignore it and enter Gambassi
Terme first.

## By train

The nearest stations are Castelfiorentino and Certaldo (both about 12 km), on the Empoli-Siena
line. From there a taxi costs about €30 (we can book it). There are a few buses from
Castelfiorentino to Gambassi Terme on weekdays, but none to the property itself.

## By plane

The nearest airports are Florence (about 60 km) and Pisa (about 85 km). Car rental is available at
both airports.
